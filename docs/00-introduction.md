# Introduction: You Can Change Careers (Really)

Welcome. This handbook is for anyone who wakes up some mornings and thinks:
*"What if I did something entirely different with my working life?"*

Throughout this handbook we follow one worked example: **Maya, a bedside
nurse** — twelve years in, good at her job, respected on her unit — who
decides she wants to become an **author and tech communicator**. Every
chapter uses Maya's pivot as the example, and then shows how *you* adapt the
same steps to your own situation, whether you're a warehouse manager, an
accountant, or a librarian.

## How to use this handbook

Work through it in order. Each chapter:

1. Explains one idea in plain English.
2. Walks Maya through it as an example.
3. Ends with a **worksheet** — something you fill in, not just read.

The chapters:

| Chapter | What it covers | Worksheet |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Why pivot | Naming what you want (and what you don't) | `pivot-reasons.md` |
| 2. Transferable skills | Finding what you already bring | `skills-inventory.md` |
| 3. Résumé rewrites with AI | Using free AI tools honestly | `resume-rewrite.md` |
| 4. Portfolio-first proof | Showing, not telling | `portfolio-plan.md` |
| 5. Affordable learning paths | Learning without going into debt | `learning-plan.md` |
| 6. The side-income ladder | Earning before you leap | `income-ladder.md` |
| 7. Impostor feelings | Pep talks for the hard weeks | `pep-talk-notes.md` |

## What this handbook will *not* do

- **No job-board spam.** We won't tell you to "apply to 200 jobs."
- **No magic.** A pivot takes months, not days. We show real timelines.
- **No condescension.** You already do a hard job. You can learn this.
- **No financial advice or medical advice.** This is education only.

## A promise

Everything here is designed to work **on your computer, for free, privately**.
Print the worksheets, fill them by hand, or keep a folder of notes. Nobody
ever sees them unless you show them.

When you're ready, turn to [Chapter 1: Why Pivot](01-why-pivot.md).
