## Honesty checklist before publishing anything

- [ ] No real employer secrets, patient data, or customer data anywhere.
- [ ] Redesign/analysis pieces use invented example content, clearly
      described as invented.
- [ ] My voice, not filler: at least one sentence in each piece only
      I could have written, drawn from lived experience.
- [ ] Where I used AI to brainstorm or edit, it is fine to keep quiet
      about it — it's my *editor*, not my author.
