# Chapter 1: Naming Why You Want to Pivot

A career pivot fails most often for one boring reason: it was never named
clearly. "I just want out of bedside" is a gust of wind. "I want to move into
technical writing about AI, on a two-year timeline, for $85k" is a sail you
can steer.

## The two questions

Answer these honestly, in writing:

1. **What am I moving *toward*?** (Not just away from.)
2. **What must the new work keep, and what can it lose?**

## Maya's version

Maya writes, in the worksheet (see `worksheets/pivot-reasons.md`):

> Moving **toward**: writing (I've co-written two unit protocols and secretly
> loved doing them), teaching patients plainly, and a growing pull toward
> explaining AI tools because I use them at home all the time.
>
> **Must keep**: hours that let me pick up my kids, some feeling of being
> *useful to humans*.
>
> **Can lose**: the shift schedule, the pager, and the thousand
> buttons on the charting screen.

Within a week, just by writing this, her fog resolved into a target: *"be a
writer who explains technology to busy, skeptical people — starting as a
side hustle, not a leap of faith."*

## Adapt it to you  — the "toward/away" trap

People often list only what they're escaping. That's the **away** list. Good —
make it — but it can't steer you, because it says nothing about direction.
Force yourself to fill the **toward** column: concrete activities you would
voluntarily do on a random Tuesday even if nobody paid you yet.

If your toward column is empty, that is real information: you may want a
*rest*, not a *pivot*. Rest first. Freedom beat. Pivot later. This handbook
will still be here.

## The two-year reality check

Most successful pivots happen gradually — a side venture grows income and
profile until the leap feels pedestrian. Maya's plan spans 24 months:

- **Months 1–3:** inventory, résumé, first pieces published.
- **Months 4–12:** small paid gigs on the side.
- **Months 13–20:** first substantial clients or a part-time hybrid role.
- **Months 21–24:** the full leap, if the numbers add up.
